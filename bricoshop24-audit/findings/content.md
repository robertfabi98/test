## 1. E-E-A-T Breakdown

### 1.1 Experience — Score: 8 / 20 (40%)

**What was found:**
- The site claims "10+ years of experience" in SERP snippets and likely on the "chi siamo" page, but this is a generic assertion with no verifiable supporting narrative (founding story, evolution of catalogue, historical milestones).
- 19,000+ eBay sales and 306 Trustpilot reviews provide indirect real-world experience signals visible off-site, but these are not surfaced or cited on-site in a structured way.
- No case studies, installation guides written from field experience, or customer project showcases are evident.
- No photo or video documentation of products in real-world settings (e.g., door installations, kitchen fits).
- Blog post "Trasmittanza termica porta blindata: cos'è e a cosa serve" suggests some topic engagement, but a single educational title cannot establish sustained first-hand experience across the catalogue.

**What is missing:**
- "Before and after" installation content or customer project galleries.
- Testimonials embedded in product/category pages (not just an external Trustpilot link).
- Staff or buyer expertise notes ("our product team recommends...").
- Founding story with specific dates, not just "10+ anni".

---

### 1.2 Expertise — Score: 10 / 25 (40%)

**What was found:**
- The porte blindate category has detailed subcategories (anta singola, doppia anta, interno multistrato), signalling catalogue depth and domain familiarity.
- At least one blog post addresses a technical concept (thermal transmittance of armored doors), which is a positive expertise signal for that vertical.
- Presence on Leroy Merlin (3,370 products) implies a supplier relationship and product knowledge sufficient for marketplace onboarding, which is a secondary expertise proxy.

**What is missing:**
- No visible author pages or named experts behind content. The September 2025 QRG explicitly weights named, credentialled authors for YMYL-adjacent content (home safety products such as armored doors qualify as YMYL-adjacent because they affect physical security).
- No technical specifications authored in-house (e.g., security class ratings, CE marking explanations, UNI EN 1627 compliance guides).
- No comparison guides ("classe 3 vs classe 4 porta blindata") that would demonstrate genuine expertise beyond product listing.
- No visible certifications, partnerships with manufacturers (Dierre, Alias, Oikos, etc.), or trade body memberships displayed.
- Single blog post is insufficient for a broad catalogue spanning DIY, garden, HVAC, and kitchen; expertise is only partially demonstrated in the door vertical.

## 2. Content Depth Assessment — Category Pages

**Assessment basis:** SERP snippet language, known category structure, USP claims.

### Homepage
- **Estimated word count:** Unknown, but USP "L'eccellenza del bricolage italiano a portata di click" and category grid suggest a lean, product-launch homepage format typical of Italian e-commerce.
- **Minimum benchmark:** 500 words.
- **Risk:** Likely at or below minimum. Standard Italian e-commerce homepages at this scale often have <300 words of indexable text. No editorial content block ("why choose us" long-form) confirmed.
- **Severity:** Medium.

### Porte Blindate (Armored Doors) — Primary Vertical
- **Positive signals:** Subcategory depth (anta singola, doppia anta, interno multistrato) implies structural organisation.
- **Gap:** Category landing pages in Italian e-commerce frequently carry only a product grid + brief intro paragraph (100-300 words). No data confirms a full editorial block covering security classes, UNI EN 1627, installation process, or buying guide content.
- **Minimum benchmark for service/category page:** 800 words of unique editorial content above the product grid.
- **Risk:** High — thin editorial layer on category pages is the most common content failure for Italian e-commerce sites competing on informational queries.

### Other Categories (arredamento, giardino, cucina, riscaldamento, climatizzazione, bricolage)
- **Risk:** Very high for thin content. With 3,370 products on Leroy Merlin alone, the product catalogue is large, but breadth does not imply editorial depth on category or subcategory landing pages.
- No evidence of buying guides, comparison articles, or editorial sections on these category pages.

## 3. Thin Content Risk — Product Listing Pages

- **Risk level:** High.
- Italian e-commerce sites at this scale routinely use manufacturer-supplied product descriptions, which are duplicated across multiple resellers.
- With 3,370 products on Leroy Merlin and 500+ on ManoMano, a significant portion of product copy is likely syndicated and therefore non-unique.
- No evidence of augmented product descriptions (installation notes, compatibility guides, user-experience addenda).
- The March 2024 Helpful Content system integration into core ranking means thin, duplicated product pages are now evaluated in every core update cycle, not only during standalone classifier runs.
- **Recommendation:** Prioritise unique value-add content on high-margin SKUs (porte blindate classe 3+, climatizzatori, cucine componibili) before attempting site-wide remediation.

## 4. Blog Quality Assessment

**Known post:** "Trasmittanza termica porta blindata: cos'è e a cosa serve"

**Positive signals:**
- Technical topic — thermal transmittance is a specific, measurable property. Addressing it shows at least surface-level vertical expertise.
- "Cos'è e a cosa serve" format (definitional + use-case) is a legitimate informational structure.

**Concerns:**
- One confirmed post title across a multi-category e-commerce site is insufficient to establish blog authority.
- Title format ("cos'è e a cosa serve") is extremely common in AI-generated Italian SEO content — it is not conclusive evidence of AI authorship, but it is a flag.
- No author byline confirmed on posts.
- No publication date visible in known data (freshness signal absent).
- No comments, social shares, or engagement signals confirmed.
- No images with descriptive alt text confirmed (schema for HowTo, FAQPage, or Article markup not confirmed).
- Minimum benchmark for a blog post is 1,500 words. Without access to the post body, compliance cannot be confirmed.

**AI-generated content risk (Sept 2025 QRG criteria):**
- Generic title structure matching common AI templates: flagged.
- No named author: flagged.
- No first-hand experience signal (e.g., "we tested door X under these conditions"): flagged.
- No unique data or original research: unknown but likely absent.
- **AI content risk: Medium-High** for the blog vertical.

## 7. Recommendations (Priority Order)

1. **[Critical] Add P.IVA and REA registration in footer** — legally required under Italian e-commerce law (D.Lgs. 70/2003) and a direct trust signal for Google's quality evaluators.
2. **[Critical] Implement AggregateRating schema** pulling Trustpilot data — unlocks star rating display in SERPs and feeds AI citation engines.
3. **[High] Create named author profile(s) for blog content** — even a single named expert with credentials for the porte blindate vertical will materially improve E-E-A-T scoring.
4. **[High] Expand blog to cover security class guide (UNI EN 1627)** — a 2,000+ word, technically accurate guide with schema (HowTo or FAQPage) would capture mid-funnel queries competitors currently own.
5. **[High] Add editorial content blocks to porte blindate category pages** — minimum 800 words covering security classes, installation process, regulatory standards, and price ranges.
6. **[Medium] Surface eBay authority on-site** — "19,000+ orders completed with 97.4% positive feedback" is a unique, verifiable trust statement that no generic e-commerce site can replicate.
7. **[Medium] Audit product descriptions for uniqueness** — prioritise top-20 SKUs by revenue; rewrite with installation tips, compatibility notes, and real-user context.
8. **[Medium] Add publication dates and author bylines to all blog posts** — basic freshness and authorship signal required by Sept 2025 QRG.
9. **[Low] Explore local landing pages** for Roma and surrounding municipalities — "porta blindata Roma" type queries have local intent that the physical address could support.
10. **[Low] Add video content** (even embedded YouTube) to category and blog pages — multimodal content signals depth and effort.
